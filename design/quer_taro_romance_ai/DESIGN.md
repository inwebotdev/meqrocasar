## Brand & Style

The design system harmonizes regional authenticity, state-level prestige, and high-conversion wedding planning mechanics. Built for couples curating destination weddings in Querétaro’s colonial haciendas, historic casonas, and vineyard corridors, the interface shifts between three coordinated aesthetic expressions:

1. **Cava & Cantera (Editorial Romance & Casonas Virreinales):** Evokes architectural heritage, cantera stone textures, private wine vaults, and artisanal opulence. High whitespace, delicate golden accents, and high-contrast editorial typography provide a sense of timeless luxury.
2. **Swipe & Play (Gamified Discovery & Romance Social):** Optimised for mobile intuition. Utilises tactile spring motions, swipeable gesture decks, and vibrant floral accents inspired by climbing bougainvillea in colonial courtyards.
3. **Ruta del Arte, Queso y Vino (Official Tourism Dossier & Warm Terroir):** Balances institutional credibility with artisanal warmth. Rich burgundy, mineral amber, and vineyard pine ground the experience with certified tourism iconography and badge layouts.

The overarching design aesthetic fuses **Minimalist Editorial** with **Tactile Glassmorphism**: raw architectural warmth paired with floating, glass-matted surfaces that preserve immersion over full-bleed photography and 9:16 vertical video reels.

## Colors

The core color system is anchored in Querétaro's natural landscape and architectural legacy. 

- **Primary (`#68182E` - Tinto Virreinal):** Deep Querétaro wine. Serves as the primary brand anchor, dominant CTAs, and structural accents across formal views.
- **Secondary (`#C06C54` - Cantera Rosa):** Weathered quarry terracotta. Used for interactive states, progress arcs, and secondary editorial callouts.
- **Tertiary (`#E9A844` - Ámbar de Bernal):** Sun-drenched monolith amber. Used for highlight accents, rating badges, match percentage indicators, and official certification markers.
- **Neutral (`#F7F4EE` - Lienzo Artesanal):** Warm linen background tone that prevents sterile digital white, evoking cotton paper and sunlit limestone walls.

### Contextual Sub-Palettes

- **Editorial Mode (Cava & Cantera):** Canvas `#FAF8F5`, Accent Gold `#D4AF37`, Foliage Green `#5B6E58`. Used during formal wedding dossier exports and venue curation viewports.
- **Discovery Mode (Swipe & Play):** Dynamic Accent `#E03E6D` (Bougainvillea Magenta) paired with Bright Pearl `#F8F6F4` and Deep Wine `#68182E`. Provides high visual energy for quiz carousels and micro-reactions.
- **Dossier & Registry Mode (Ruta del Arte, Queso y Vino):** Deep Burgundy `#4A1525`, Vineyard Pine `#2F4839`, Amber `#E9A844`. Deployed for certified supplier seals, regional certificates, and terroir index dashboards.

## Typography

The type scale combines the editorial heritage of **Playfair Display** with the clear, high-density legibility of **Plus Jakarta Sans**. 

- **Playfair Display** is restricted to level headings, match percentages, key milestone accolades, and editorial quote pullouts. In running copy, italicized variants should be reserved strictly for Spanish regional expressions (e.g., *Casonas Virreinales*, *Pueblos Mágicos*, *Vendimia*).
- **Plus Jakarta Sans** powers interactive workflows, UI labels, dashboard metrics, card details, and quiz choice chips.
- Set uppercase tracking (`letter-spacing: 0.08em`) on `label-sm` when designating official tourism validation stamps, venue tags, or vendor categories.

## Layout & Spacing

The interface employs a fluid-column system calibrated for mobile-first romantic discovery, expanding to an editorial grid on larger screens.

- **Mobile (< 768px):** Single-column layout with 4-column subgrids for metrics. Edge margins are fixed at `1rem`, with swipe-deck cards stretching to full safe width minus `margin`. Gaps utilize `space-md` (16px) vertically.
- **Tablet (768px - 1024px):** 8-column grid with `margin-tablet` (32px) margins and `gutter` (16px). Used primarily for two-panel comparison views: AI recommendations on the left, live moodboard canvas on the right.
- **Desktop (> 1024px):** 12-column layout capped at a maximum width of `1360px` with `margin-desktop` (48px) and `gutter-desktop` (24px).
- **Special Viewport Constraints:**
  - **Moodboard Preview (1200x630):** Enforces a locked 1.91:1 canvas aspect ratio for social export and dossier summaries, maintaining an internal 24px grid edge.
  - **Reel Player (9:16):** Locked vertical presentation with bottom-anchored content scrims, maintaining a 72px thumb-safe interaction zone at the base.

## Elevation & Depth

Visual hierarchy uses warm, diffuse ambient lighting and frosted layered glass to reference natural stone and sunlit vineyard air.

- **Level 1 (Subtle Base Panels):** `box-shadow: 0 2px 8px -2px rgba(104, 24, 46, 0.04), 0 1px 4px -1px rgba(74, 21, 37, 0.02)`. Used for inactive cards, list items, and form inputs on natural linen canvas.
- **Level 2 (Interactive Floating Elements):** `box-shadow: 0 8px 24px -4px rgba(104, 24, 46, 0.08), 0 4px 12px -2px rgba(192, 108, 84, 0.06)`. Applied to active deck cards, quick-filter bars, and venue thumbnails.
- **Level 3 (Modals, Overlays & Quiz Decks):** `box-shadow: 0 16px 40px -8px rgba(74, 21, 37, 0.16), 0 8px 16px -4px rgba(104, 24, 46, 0.08)`. Applied to swipeable cards in the active interaction position.
- **Frosted Glassmorphism (Cantera Glass):** `background: rgba(247, 244, 238, 0.82); backdrop-filter: blur(12px); border: 1px solid rgba(212, 175, 55, 0.25)`. Deployed across the persistent bottom navigation, video reel controllers, and floating moodboard tags.

## Shapes

The shape system adopts Level 2 (Rounded) corner geometry, referencing traditional Spanish archways and colonial carved cantera molding:

- **Base Components (0.5rem / 8px):** Chips, data badges, text fields, mini action buttons.
- **Containers & Surfaces (1rem / 16px):** Wedding match cards, quiz options, itinerary modules, venue profile wrappers.
- **Focal Objects & Dialogs (1.5rem / 24px):** Primary swipeable cards, moodboard previews, floating filter sheets.
- **Pill Exceptions (`9999px`):** Floating filter pills, official tourism certified badges, video reel tags, and primary action buttons.

## Components

### 1. Buttons
- **Primary ('Tinto Virreinal'):** Background `#68182E`, text `#FAF8F5`, height 48px, corner radius `9999px`. Subtle gold accent border (`1px solid rgba(212, 175, 55, 0.4)`) on focus or hover.
- **Secondary ('Cantera Soft'):** Background `rgba(192, 108, 84, 0.12)`, text `#C06C54`, border `1px solid rgba(192, 108, 84, 0.3)`.
- **Gamified Accent ('Bougainvillea Pop'):** Background `#E03E6D`, text `#FFFFFF`, elevation Level 2. Used exclusively within the Swipe & Play quiz module for positive affirmation actions.

### 2. Quiz Card Carousel & Swipe Decks
- Stacked card interface with `24px` corner radiuses and subtle aspect ratio scaling (active: 100%, next: 95%, background: 90%).
- Integrated gesture indicators: subtle right-tilt reveals a bougainvillea ring overlay (`#E03E6D`), left-tilt reveals neutral slate dismiss tone.
- Floating bottom controller bar using Frosted Glassmorphism with Pass (X), Save (Heart), and Detail (Info) buttons.

### 3. Result Dashboards & Match Percentages
- AI-calculated wedding match badges rendered as circular SVG strokes: path color `#E9A844` on a `#68182E` track.
- Breakdown split across 3 criteria: Architectural Vibe, Wine & Gastronomy, Guest Accessibility.
- Cards utilize clean linen cards (`#FFFFFF` on `#F7F4EE` canvas) with a 1px border of `#E5DFD5`.

### 4. Official Tourism Badges ('SECTUR Querétaro')
- Border: `1px solid rgba(233, 168, 68, 0.5)`.
- Background: `rgba(74, 21, 37, 0.04)`.
- Typography: `label-sm` in all-caps tracking, prepended with a gold seal or agave/cantera vector silhouette.

### 5. Moodboard Preview (1200x630 Export)
- Dynamic CSS grid conforming to a 1.91:1 ratio.
- Layout: 4-cell asymmetrical bento featuring:
  - 1 primary venue hero cell (60% width).
  - 2 stacked detail cells (floral/table decor, wine/cheese pairing).
  - 1 typography summary card cell in deep `#4A1525` with gold foil accents stating couple names, selected Pueblo Mágico, and official QR verification stamp.

### 6. Vertical Video Reel Player (9:16)
- Full bleed presentation with an edge-to-edge container.
- Bottom gradient scrim: linear gradient from transparent at 50% to `rgba(20, 10, 12, 0.85)` at 100%.
- Interactive overlays: right-hand vertical rail featuring vendor profile avatar, audio track tag (*Son Queretano / Classical Strings*), and direct booking inquiry pill.

### 7. Form Inputs & Selection Chips
- Text inputs set on `#FFFFFF` with an inset `1px` border of `rgba(104, 24, 46, 0.2)`. Label typography uses `label-md`. Focused state replaces border with `#68182E` and a 2px outer ring of `#E9A844` at 30% opacity.
- Multi-select chips use a pill shape, alternating between `#F7F4EE` unselected and `#68182E` selected with white label text.
